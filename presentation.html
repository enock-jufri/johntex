The Problem & Our Solution

✖ The Challenges

  • Manual payroll calculations
    Spreadsheets are error-prone and time-consuming when computing gross pay, advances, and net amounts for dozens of workers every week.
  • No real-time visibility
    Workers don't know their earnings, advances taken, or wallet balance until payday. Disputes are common.
  • Cash payments are messy
    Handling cash for every worker is slow, insecure, and leaves no digital record of who was paid when.
  • No audit trail
    Without tracking who approved what and when, financial discrepancies are impossible to investigate.

✔ The Solution

  • Automated weekly payroll
    One-click payroll run computes gross pay from approved production logs, deducts outstanding advances, and produces net pay for every active worker.
  • Real-time worker wallets
    Every worker gets a live wallet showing total earned, advances taken, paid out, and current balance — updated instantly on every transaction.
  • M-Pesa B2C integration
    Pay workers individually or in bulk via Safaricom M-Pesa. Real-time callback updates payment status. Cash payments also supported.
  • Complete audit logging
    Every action — login, approval, payment, config change — is logged with user, IP, location, and timestamp for full traceability.

Key Features

🧵

Production Tracking

Log daily garment pieces per worker by product type. Auto-calculates earnings from piece rates. Date-filtered views with search and pagination.

📊

Payroll Automation

Run weekly payroll with one click. Computes gross pay from approved production, deducts unpaid advances, and generates net pay for every active worker.

📱

M-Pesa Integration

Safaricom B2C v3 API for sending payments to worker phones. Single and bulk pay. Async callback handling updates payment status automatically.

💰

Worker Wallet

Real-time balance ledger per worker: total earned, advances taken, total paid, and current balance. Full transaction history with credit/debit/payout entries.

✅

Approval Workflow

Production logs and advance requests require supervisor approval. Single and bulk approve. Role-based: clerks enter, supervisors approve, admins pay.

📈

Analytics Dashboard

Revenue trends, product mix breakdown, worker productivity rankings, production volume charts, and financial summaries. CSV export for payroll data.
